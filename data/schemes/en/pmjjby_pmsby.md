# PMJJBY and PMSBY (Jan Suraksha insurance schemes)

## Overview
PMJJBY (life insurance) and PMSBY (accident insurance) were launched in May 2015 as low-cost insurance schemes for people with a bank or post office account. Both are one-year covers from 1 June to 31 May and are renewable every year through auto-debit.

## PMJJBY Benefits
Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana provides a life cover of Rs 2 lakh in case of death of the insured due to any reason. The premium is Rs 436 per year, auto-debited from the account holder's bank account.

## PMJJBY Eligibility
People aged 18 to 50 years who have a savings bank or post office account and give consent for auto-debit can join. A person who joins before turning 50 can continue the cover up to the age of 55 by paying the premium.

## PMSBY Benefits
Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana provides accident insurance: Rs 2 lakh for accidental death or total and irrecoverable disability (loss of both eyes, both hands or feet, or one eye and one hand or foot), and Rs 1 lakh for partial permanent disability (loss of one eye or one hand or foot). The premium is only Rs 20 per year.

## PMSBY Eligibility
People aged 18 to 70 years who have a savings bank or post office account and give consent for auto-debit can join.

## How to Apply
Fill the consent-cum-declaration form at the bank or post office branch, or enrol through internet banking, mobile banking or the Jan Suraksha portal. Aadhaar is the primary KYC document.
