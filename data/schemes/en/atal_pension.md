# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

## Overview
Atal Pension Yojana was launched in May 2015 to provide old-age income security, especially to workers in the unorganised sector. It is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).

## Benefits
- A guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000 or Rs 5,000 from the age of 60, depending on the contribution chosen.
- After the death of the subscriber, the spouse receives the same pension for life.
- After the death of both the subscriber and the spouse, the accumulated pension corpus is returned to the nominee.
- Contributions are eligible for tax benefit under Section 80CCD.

## Eligibility
- Any citizen of India aged between 18 and 40 years.
- The applicant must have a savings bank account or a post office savings account.
- From 1 October 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer is not eligible to join APY.

## Contribution
The monthly contribution depends on the age at joining and the pension amount chosen. For example, a person joining at age 18 pays about Rs 42 per month for a pension of Rs 1,000, while a person joining at 40 pays about Rs 291 per month for the same pension. Contributions are auto-debited from the bank account monthly, quarterly or half-yearly, and the subscriber contributes until the age of 60.

## Documents Required
Savings bank account, Aadhaar number (recommended) and an active mobile number.

## How to Apply
Fill the APY registration form at the bank or post office branch where the savings account is held, or register through the bank's internet or mobile banking.
