# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

## Overview
MGNREGA, enacted in 2005, is a demand-driven wage employment programme that gives a legal guarantee of work to rural households. It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas and create durable assets such as ponds, roads and water conservation structures.

## Benefits
- At least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
- Work must be provided within 15 days of applying; otherwise the applicant is entitled to an unemployment allowance.
- Work is to be provided within a radius of 5 km of the village; if it is farther, an extra 10 percent wage is paid.
- Wages are paid directly into bank or post office accounts within 15 days of the work, at the notified State-wise wage rate.
- At least one-third of the beneficiaries must be women. Work-site facilities such as drinking water, shade and a creche are provided.

## Eligibility
- The household must reside in a rural area.
- Adult members (18 years or older) who are willing to do unskilled manual work can apply.
- The household must register with the local Gram Panchayat and obtain a Job Card.

## Documents Required
Application for registration with names of adult members, age, address, and photographs; Aadhaar and bank or post office account details for wage payment.

## How to Apply
Submit a written or oral application for registration to the Gram Panchayat. A Job Card is issued within 15 days after verification. Then apply for work in writing to the Gram Panchayat or Programme Officer, specifying the period of work needed (at least 14 days).
