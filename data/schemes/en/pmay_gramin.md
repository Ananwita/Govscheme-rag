# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Overview
PMAY-G was launched in November 2016 (restructured from the Indira Awaas Yojana) to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to rural households who are houseless or living in kutcha and dilapidated houses.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance of Rs 1.20 lakh per house in plain areas and Rs 1.30 lakh in hilly States, difficult areas and IAP districts.
- Minimum house size of 25 square metres, including a dedicated area for hygienic cooking.
- Additional support of 90 to 95 person-days of unskilled labour wages under MGNREGA, and Rs 12,000 for a toilet through Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) or other sources.
- Convergence with other schemes for electricity, LPG (Ujjwala) and drinking water connections.
- The beneficiary may also take a bank loan of up to Rs 70,000.

## Eligibility
Beneficiaries are identified from the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 housing deprivation data and the Awaas+ survey, and the list is verified by the Gram Sabha. Priority is given to:
- Houseless households.
- Households living in houses with zero, one or two rooms with kutcha walls and kutcha roof.
Within these, SC/ST households, minorities and other categories are prioritised as per scheme guidelines. Households that already own a pucca house, or that meet the exclusion criteria notified in the Awaas+ survey (such as having a government-employee member or paying income tax), are not eligible.

## How to Apply
There is no direct online application. Eligible households are selected from the Permanent Wait List prepared from SECC 2011 and Awaas+ data and approved by the Gram Sabha. People can contact their Gram Panchayat or Block office, and can check their status on pmayg.nic.in using the registration number. Funds are released in instalments directly into the beneficiary's bank account after geo-tagged inspection of construction stages.
