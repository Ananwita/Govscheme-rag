# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched in May 2016 to provide clean cooking fuel (LPG) to women from poor households, replacing unclean fuels such as firewood, coal and cow-dung cakes that harm health. The second phase, Ujjwala 2.0, was launched in August 2021 and also covers migrant families.

## Benefits
- A deposit-free LPG connection in the name of an adult woman of the household. The Government provides cash assistance of Rs 1,600 for a 14.2 kg single-bottle connection (Rs 1,150 for a 5 kg connection).
- Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill and the hot plate (stove) are provided free of cost along with the connection.
- PMUY beneficiaries receive a targeted subsidy of Rs 300 per 14.2 kg cylinder for up to 12 refills per year.

## Eligibility
The applicant must be a woman who has attained 18 years of age and belongs to a poor household. There must be no other LPG connection in the same household. She must belong to one of the following categories:
- Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes households.
- Beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin).
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households.
- Most Backward Classes, forest dwellers, residents of islands and river islands, and tea and ex-tea garden tribes.
- Households listed in SECC 2011.
- Any other poor household that submits a 14-point declaration of poverty.

## Documents Required
eKYC of the applicant woman, Aadhaar card of the applicant and of all adult members of the family, ration card or a family composition document (migrants may give a self-declaration), and bank account details with IFSC.

## How to Apply
Apply online on the PMUY portal (pmuy.gov.in) by choosing a distributor of Indane, Bharat Gas or HP Gas, or visit the nearest LPG distributor with the documents.

## Helpline
LPG helpline: 1906 (emergency) and 1800-233-3555 (toll-free).
