# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)

## Overview
AB PM-JAY, launched in September 2018, is one of the largest publicly funded health assurance schemes in the world. It is implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA) and provides cashless and paperless access to hospital treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals across India.

## Benefits
The scheme provides a health cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. It covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalisation and 15 days of post-hospitalisation expenses, including diagnostics and medicines. There is no cap on family size or age of members, and all pre-existing diseases are covered from the first day. Benefits are portable across the country, so a beneficiary can get treatment in any empanelled hospital in any State.

## Eligibility
Eligibility is entitlement-based and is decided on the basis of deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011).
- Rural households: families falling under deprivation criteria D1, D2, D3, D4, D5 and D7, such as households with only one room with kucha walls and roof, households with no adult member aged 16 to 59, female-headed households with no adult male member aged 16 to 59, households with a disabled member and no able-bodied adult, SC/ST households, and landless households deriving a major part of income from manual casual labour. Destitute households, those living on alms, manual scavenger families, primitive tribal groups and legally released bonded labourers are automatically included.
- Urban households: 11 occupational categories of workers, such as rag pickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, plumbers, masons, painters, security guards, transport workers, sanitation workers and home-based workers.
- Senior citizens: since October 2024, all senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for a separate cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per year under the Ayushman Vay Vandana card, irrespective of their income.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card or another government photo identity document, and a ration card or other proof of family. The Ayushman card is generated after Aadhaar-based eKYC.

## How to Apply
There is no enrolment process for the base scheme. A person can check whether their family is eligible on the beneficiary portal (beneficiary.nha.gov.in) or the Ayushman app using their mobile number, or by visiting an empanelled hospital (Ayushman Mitra help desk) or a Common Service Centre. Eligible beneficiaries then create an Ayushman card through eKYC.
