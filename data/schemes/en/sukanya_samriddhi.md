# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small savings scheme for the girl child, launched in January 2015 under the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign. It helps parents save for the education and marriage of their daughter.

## Benefits
- The scheme offers one of the highest interest rates among small savings schemes. The rate is notified by the Government every quarter (8.2 percent per annum in recent quarters) and is compounded yearly.
- Deposits qualify for income tax deduction under Section 80C. The interest earned and the maturity amount are also tax free.

## Eligibility
- The account can be opened by a natural or legal guardian in the name of a girl child who is below 10 years of age.
- Only one account is allowed per girl child.
- A maximum of two accounts can be opened in a family (for two girl children). A third account is allowed only in case of twins or triplets.
- The girl child must be a resident Indian.

## Deposit Rules
The minimum deposit is Rs 250 in a financial year and the maximum is Rs 1.5 lakh in a financial year. Deposits must be made for 15 years from the date of opening the account. If the minimum deposit is not made, the account becomes a defaulted account, which can be revived by paying a penalty of Rs 50 per year of default.

## Withdrawal and Maturity
The account matures 21 years after the date of opening. Up to 50 percent of the balance at the end of the previous financial year can be withdrawn for the girl's education after she turns 18 or passes Class 10. The account can also be closed after the girl turns 18 for the purpose of her marriage.

## Documents Required
Birth certificate of the girl child, identity proof and address proof of the parent or guardian (such as Aadhaar and PAN), and passport-size photographs.

## How to Apply
Visit any post office or an authorised bank branch (such as SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB, ICICI or HDFC), fill the account opening form and make the initial deposit.
