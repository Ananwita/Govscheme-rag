# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana was launched in April 2015 to provide collateral-free loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises. MUDRA stands for Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency.

## Loan Categories and Benefits
- Shishu: loans up to Rs 50,000.
- Kishore: loans above Rs 50,000 and up to Rs 5 lakh.
- Tarun: loans above Rs 5 lakh and up to Rs 10 lakh.
- Tarun Plus: loans above Rs 10 lakh and up to Rs 20 lakh, for entrepreneurs who have availed and successfully repaid earlier loans under the Tarun category (announced in the Union Budget 2024-25).
No collateral or third-party guarantee is required; loans are covered under the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU). Interest rates are decided by the lending institution as per RBI guidelines.

## Eligibility
Any Indian citizen who has a business plan for an income-generating activity in manufacturing, trading or services, including activities allied to agriculture (such as poultry, dairy and beekeeping), and whose credit need is up to Rs 20 lakh. Small shopkeepers, vendors, artisans, food processing units, repair shops and small manufacturers are typical borrowers. The applicant should not be a defaulter to any bank or financial institution.

## Documents Required
Identity and address proof, proof of business identity and address, quotation of machinery or items to be purchased, photographs, and category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/minority) if applicable.

## How to Apply
Apply at any bank, Regional Rural Bank, Small Finance Bank, Micro Finance Institution or NBFC, or online through the Udyam Mitra portal (udyamimitra.in) or JanSamarth portal.
